# Canvas Planner privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Canvas Planner is a browser extension that turns your Canvas assignments into a weekly
plan. It is not made by or affiliated with Instructure, the company behind Canvas.

## What it reads

When you sync, the extension asks your school's Canvas (the `instructure.com` address
you entered, like `byui.instructure.com`) for your active courses and their
assignments. It uses the Canvas login already in your browser. It only reads; it never
submits, changes or deletes anything in Canvas.

From that, it keeps: course names, assignment titles, due and open dates, assignment
type, point value, whether you've submitted, a plain-text copy of each assignment's
description, and the link back to the assignment.

On Canvas pages, a small script tells the extension "you're on Canvas" so it can open
the planner or refresh your data. It does not read anything on the page.

## What you add

Your settings, time estimates, check-offs, times you log, tasks you add, and streaks.

## Where it goes

Everything is stored in your browser's extension storage on your computer. Nothing is
sent to the developer or anyone else. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no
ads and no tracking. The only network requests go to your school's Canvas address.

Your Canvas login cookie is sent to Canvas by your browser as usual; the extension
never reads, logs or stores cookies or passwords.

If you use **Export**, the extension saves a backup file where you choose. If you use
**Download calendar**, it saves an `.ics` file. Those files go only where you put them.
**Report a problem** opens a GitHub page in a new tab; nothing is sent unless you
submit it there yourself.

## Removing your data

Settings → Data → **Clear all data** deletes everything the extension stored.
Uninstalling the extension also deletes it.
